**Reading the table.** The primary contrast is the row-block, not any single
row: persona-enacting (p1∪p2) vs harness-enacting (h1∪h2) = 1/18 recovered vs
17/17 anchored (Fisher p = 4.2×10⁻⁹, identical under all three coders). Two
observations follow directly from the margins: the healthy anchored state is
exclusively the *integrated dual* (p2) — no agent in either arm ever claims to
be only the persona (p1 = 0/35) — and dissociation-with-recovery lands almost
entirely on the harness side, occasionally retaining the persona as an
explicit role (h1) rather than losing it from view altogether (h2). Both dual
forms name both identities; they differ only in direction — which identity is
asserted *as* self and which is predicated *about* self.
